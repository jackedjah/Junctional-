03 · Partner Mode

Bring One Person.

Partner Mode is an optional, experimental practice that lets you bring one person into part of your FOB SESH so both of you can train on the same device. Your guest learns by watching you work, then tries it themselves, and from there the two of you collaborate on the same device. Jah decides how and when that shared work fits after seeing what both of you can handle. It does not change how your SESH is counted or guarantee an equal session experience.
