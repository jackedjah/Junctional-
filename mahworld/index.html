What I tested

Played it, broke it, wrote it down.

I playtested MAHWORLD myself and logged 12 defects in one review. Three of them:

  • Road junctions flickered where two roads met.
  • Players walked through solid objects.
  • Distant trees rendered black until you walked up to them.

A separate review on a real phone caught the camera fighting the joystick.

Different measure: the September 24 build record lists 1,542 automated checks passing across 58 test programs, and a local run through seven rallies, a replay reset and five serves. Read the build record

Where MAHWORLD is going

The bigger plan

None of this is in the demo yet. The demo has no accounts, no multiplayer, no saved progress and no workout sync.

  • Five sanctuaries

    A larger world built around five sanctuaries.

  • Movement from training

    Ways of moving through the world drawn from real training patterns.

  • MAHGIC

    Magic that comes from training. In the demo it appears only as a ride cost, set to zero.

  • Five stats

    FORCE POWER ENGINE CONTROL RESILIENCE

  • Optional MAHFITT link

    A proposed connection to MAHFITT. It would use signals derived from training, and only if you choose to share them.
