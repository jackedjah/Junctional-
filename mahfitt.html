Your training system, while you are actually training.

MAHFITT is the private member companion built around FOB Systems training. It keeps the workout, movement references, music, progress, meals, scheduling and selected health summaries inside one coherent interface instead of scattering them across separate apps.

01

Live Workout

TRACK THE WORK

Move exercise by exercise, record sets and notes, see workout completion, and keep progress connected to the exact movement.

SEE THE MOVEMENT

Supported exercises include quick start/peak demonstration references. When a verified visual is not available, MAHFITT shows the real movement profile instead of substituting the wrong exercise.

MUSIC STAYS WITH YOU

Theme and MAH PLAYER controls are designed to remain available through the training flow without creating a second competing player.

02

Beyond The Set Counter

MAH PLAYER

Private audio, playlists, speed, pitch, effects, song navigation and workout-aware playback controls.

MEAL GRADE

Photo-assisted meal logging with private storage controls, nutrition-oriented scoring and a persistent member log.

MAH CALENDAR

Member scheduling, purchased-session visibility and training logistics inside the same account.

MAH HEALTH

Optional supported health summaries can be imported without turning MAHFITT into a raw health-data warehouse.

THEMES

Color, atmosphere and music can change together so the interface feels personal while the underlying controls remain consistent.

PROGRESS

Training history, exercise-specific progress and Month Wrapped summaries make the record useful after the workout ends.

03

Built As A Private Member Tool

The public page you are reading is intentionally separate from the member app. Training records, member tools and authenticated routes remain private and are marked not to be indexed by search engines.
