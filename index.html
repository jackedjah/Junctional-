An Original Training Practice

FOB was built from the ground up.

FOB was created by Jah Matt, @jahfobin on IG, who studied Kinesiology for a decade while coaching in gyms, physical therapy clinics, and his own private training. He built and tested the FOBand himself, so the equipment and the method were developed simultaneously. New movements and progressions are still being developed.

For now FOB can only be performed through supervised sessions. Jah has made it a point to gamify the session structure, so as you learn to control the FOBand you’re also having an enjoyable experience.

Questions

Before you book.

Do I need to be fit or experienced?

No. FOB scales to every level and your first session is coached from the first rep. Nobody is thrown in.

Is this a gym membership?

No. FOB is not a traditional gym membership or recurring subscription. Private FOB SESH purchasing is a members-only access experience: once your session request is confirmed, your approved full name unlocks the private SESH purchase portal. You purchase SESH packages as needed, with no recurring membership dues. FOB comes to your approved outdoor training location, using spaces with enough room to coach the equipment safely.

Do I need to play a sport?

No. FOB is for general fitness, strength, conditioning, body control, and athletic performance. Sessions are adjusted to your experience and goals, whether you are beginning to exercise, already train regularly, or play a competitive sport.

What should I bring?

Bring water and a basic work glove. Wear comfortable, breathable clothing you can move in. Athletic shoes are preferred; boots can work with modified footwork. The FOBallast is provided.

How long is a private FOB session?

Private FOB sessions generally last between 45 and 75 minutes. Exact duration may depend on setup, instruction, current progression, and the amount of controlled training appropriate for the session.

How does payment work?

Once your session request is approved and you are added to the FOB member system, your approved name unlocks the private FOB SESH purchasing portal. Your available SESH packages, any location-based travel adjustment, and other important purchase details are shown there before you confirm payment.

Are group classes available?

Small-group FOB classes are offered through scheduled cohorts with limited capacity. Current group pricing and availability are shown inside the group access flow when a cohort is open.
