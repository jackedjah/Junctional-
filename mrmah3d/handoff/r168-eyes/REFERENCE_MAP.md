# R168 Reference Map

## 01 — CANONICAL_EYE_SELECTION_WELCOMING_PIXEL
**Role:** Primary eye-catalog direction.

Use for:
- friendly/everyday proportion of the library
- pixel/faceted construction
- black face-display field
- cyan/ice-blue eye color
- normal/round/almond/soft/warm eye shapes
- special eyes as a minority/secondary band

Do not copy the sheet literally as the final mobile layout.

## 02 — CANONICAL_SIGNATURE_FACES
**Role:** Primary signature-face and expression direction.

Use for:
- Mr. Mah signature baseline
- Mrs. Mah signature baseline
- expression range
- malleable width/open/close/gaze concept
- face identity hierarchy

## 03 — EYE_SYSTEM_IMPLEMENTATION_BOARD
**Role:** System architecture reference.

Use for:
- explicit EYES step in creator
- preset atlas concept
- modular base/iris/detail layers
- implementation mindset

Treat any text/code shown inside the artwork as conceptual, not authoritative source code.

## 04 — LESS_ANIME_UI_DIRECTION
**Role:** Transitional visual reference.

Use for:
- reduced anime styling
- controlled pixel geometry
- black-field integration

Superseded by 01 where 04 looks too uniformly aggressive.

## 05 / 06 / 07 — MR./MRS. FACE REFERENCES
**Role:** Canonical material/head integration.

Use for:
- face-display area location
- crystal head framing
- blue/black/platinum palette
- scale relationship between eyes and head

Do not inherit the old giant circular eyes literally. The whole purpose of R168 is to evolve them.
