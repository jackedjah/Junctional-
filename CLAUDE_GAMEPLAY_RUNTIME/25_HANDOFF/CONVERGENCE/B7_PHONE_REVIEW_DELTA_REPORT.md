## MONSTER TARGETING
Result: **25 / 25** on `deploy/joba/probe_targeting.mjs --mobile` (a real CDP touch tap on the Dogkie and the horse; the fish and the phoenix are tapped in-page through the same pointer path because a 13–16 m/s body outruns the probe's round trips) — Dogkie / fish / horse (passive, it feints and never damages) / phoenix all lock through `tapAt → entityAt → LOCK_TARGET`, the lock marker follows the creature figure, slot 1 damages THE locked body through the one authority, the lock clears on KO and on range loss. What was actually wrong on the phone, fixed:
- **the tap was evaluated at finger-LIFT**: a launching fish or a diving phoenix had left the point by then → the tap became a destination. Now the touch layer remembers what the finger LANDED on (`hit0`) and the lock goes to that body (mouse path too).
- **line of sight was 2-D**: a flyer and a phoenix over the highland were "blocked" by the monoliths below them. `Colliders.segHitsShape` now compares a shape's TOP (base + height) with the sight line, and `RulesField` runs the line at the LOWER body's height + 1 m (`losHeight`); ground pairs keep the 1.0 m rule.
- **nothing to hit**: the fish wound up on a 4.2 m orbit and the phoenix only ever passed through in a 0.3 s dive — both outside a body strike's 2.5 m / ±1.6 m reach. Data-driven: FISH `combat.swirl_radius_m 2.3` (inside reach between launches); PHOENIX `combat.sweep_s 1.4 / sweep_radius_m 2.2` — a SWEEP pass at the player's height after each dive (the owner's "sweeping physical-MAHGIC pass"), then it climbs for the next anticipation. Host test 11 / 12: the strikes land.
- **could not look up at it**: the camera pitch floor is `pitch_min_flight −1.05` while airborne (ground keeps −0.4).
- the dev summon strikes the phoenix NEAREST the player (test + probe determinism); creature / wildlife figures get the lock marker (`play.js` reads `statusOf` positions); `P.camDrag` / `P.adapterGet` / `worldB.registry()` dev accessors.
