28 — PERFORMANCE BUDGETS: HIGH QUALITY WITHOUT A FALSE PROMISE
==========================================================================

Do not promise identical offline-render quality on every phone or claim current
FPS from the recording. Establish actual device/browser/GPU/canvas/tier and input
scenario for measurements. Keep source resolution, runtime resolution and display

Proposed candidate targets, NOT measured results:
- Supported representative PC: stable 60 fps goal with measured frame tails.
- Supported representative phone: 60 fps ambition; a stable 30 fps quality tier
  is the minimum planning floor for supported low hardware, not approval at 19fps.
- No multi-second active-play main-thread block, runaway memory, tab reload,
  repeated shader compilation storm or visible floor failure.
If the actual device cannot meet the selected tier, simplify costs before adding
more replicated detail and record the supported profile honestly.

A 60 fps frame is 16.67 ms; 30 fps is 33.33 ms. Measure median plus p95/p99 frame
intervals, long stalls and interactions, not only an average. CPU emulation does
not emulate phone GPU limits/thermal behavior. Separate JS heap, estimated texture
residency and other GPU costs. Renderer texture counts are not byte-accurate VRAM.

Pixel budget matters: multiplying device pixel ratio by 3 yields ~9x pixels for
a fixed CSS canvas. A proposed cap uses scale=min(deviceDPR,sqrt(Pmax/(Wcss*Hcss))).
Choose Pmax/tier from measured sharpness/performance, not blindly force DPR3. Keep
text/UI crisp separately. A first low/mid experiment around 0.8-1.2 megapixel render
area is a tuning proposal, not universal quality law. Only resize on actual changes.

Measure cost per subsystem: creature animation/AI, draw submissions, visible
triangles, materials/program variants, shadows, transparency/transmission, water,
clouds, UI, texture residency, loading and region activation. Instancing doesn't
remove fragment/vertex work. Frozen offscreen geometry isn't freed memory.

Maintain/strengthen B8's bounded texture tier (reported ~190 MB) with comparable
measurement; don't blindly call this a universal Safari limit. Near hero textures,
shared compressed maps, per-region residency and animation LOD are preferable to
a uniform drastic blur. Far geometry/meadows/cloud layers/creatures simplify by
visibility and significance with hysteresis to avoid popping or repeated respawn.

Use pooled bounded particles, few shadow casters, shared clocks and infrequent
distant AI. High settings add measured refinement, not different gameplay rules.
No optional quality setting may break targeting, hide necessary enemies, change
combat cost, shrink resource-node hitboxes or make controls unusable.
