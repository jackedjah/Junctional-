## Blockers / honest residuals
1. **Rigs:** the horse and phoenix GLBs are unrigged in effect (root-only weights) — real limb / wing articulation needs a weighted rig from the asset lane (Character / Tripo re-rig); the shader deformation is a bounded stand-in and is documented as such in the registry. The fish body has no spine weights (shader undulation).
2. **Landing on the sea / islets:** the host ground beyond the beach ring is still y 0 (no sea collider) — a player who flies out and lands over the sea or on a reachable islet stands on the host's flat ground, not on the plateau / water. A water / islet collider set is the next registry step (same generator).
3. **Far islands read monochrome** in the cool haze fog beyond ~300 m (family tints applied to the spires, muted by the fog) — an atmospheric choice that could be tuned (fog far / tint mix) on owner review.
4. **Perf on a phone is unmeasured** (emulation only); the desktop frame-time probe is relative.
5. The fish are invisible under the opaque sea except when breaching (deep water by design); the canal has no fish (it is 0.3 m deep).
6. Pet acquisition / wild-encounter interaction: metadata only (owner decision), no interaction hooks yet.
