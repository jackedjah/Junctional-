MAHWORLD — ASSISTANT'S 20% CREATIVE INPUT

The user's core vision remains authoritative. These are supporting design decisions intended to turn that vision into a coherent world rather than a collection of disconnected features.

1. WORLD SPINE + LOOPS
Create a readable main traversal spine from the central civic/plaza area into major regions, then use loops and side paths for exploration. The player should understand where the world wants them to go without being trapped in a corridor.

2. THREE PATH HIERARCHIES
A. Crown Causeways — broad, unmistakable main routes between major regions.
B. Regional Paths — narrower paths connecting local landmarks and habitats.
C. Discovery Trails — optional paths leading to rare views, creature pockets, and future challenge locations.

All should feel like MAHWORLD: faceted crystal structure, satin platinum framing, restrained emissive seams, and biome color accents.

3. COLOR WITHOUT RAINBOW NOISE
Keep platinum/chromium/graphite as the world-wide material family. Red, pink, gold, green, and purple should appear as region-dominant crystal/mineral energy families rather than painted surfaces. Use color in crystal veins, geological growths, canopy extremities, path cores, structural accents, atmospheric particles, and landmark lighting.

4. REGION LOGIC
Use geography to prepare future progression without implementing quest gates yet:
- Central Platinum Nexus: social/civic hub and orientation point.
- Cobalt/Umbral Forest: Dogkie ecology and darker dense traversal.
- Prismatic Open Range: open land and isolated horse encounters.
- Crown Spires / Aerial Highlands: cliffs, towers, elevated routes, phoenix territory.
- Luminous Coast / Deepwater: fish activity, swimming-facing geography, deeper offshore ecology.

These are design roles, not mandatory final names.

5. WATER SHOULD EARN ITS SPACE
Do not simply delete the water. Increase/reshape land until the water reads as intentional geography: bays, channels, lakes, shoreline basins, deeper outer water, bridges, coves. The high-level view should feel land-rich enough that water is a feature rather than empty filler.

6. TREE MATERIAL SPLIT
The user's new tree rule is strong: make the structural trunk and main boughs readable metal, then save crystal complexity for extremities. Add only restrained MAHWORLD texture to the metal: shallow longitudinal faceting/grooves, soft heat-grey tonal variation, clean bevels, optional hairline biome-colored energy seams. Avoid natural bark and avoid mirror-chrome noise.

7. SKY DEPTH THROUGH LAYERS
Use huge Sun/Moon, high cloud layer, lower cloud/mist layer, horizon atmosphere, distant silhouette fade, and restrained crystal/energy particles. Add a shared wind vector that can influence cloud drift, selected vegetation motion, distant particles, and aerial creature feel. This creates a coherent world without expensive full volumetrics.

8. FUTURE-PROOF SURFACES
Let terrain expose tags/metadata such as ground, crystal path, forest floor, shallow water, deep water, rock, bridge, and special surface. JOB B does not rewrite player locomotion, but these tags allow JOB A to later drive footsteps, attacks, swim behavior, climbing, VFX, and sound correctly.

9. MOBILE-FIRST 'AHEAD OF TIME'
Ahead-of-time quality should come from coherent systems, not brute-force polygons: chunked loading, instancing, LODs, culling, pooled effects, compressed assets where supported, stable materials, responsive water near the player, cheaper representations at distance, and landmark-driven composition.

10. CREATURES SHOULD TEACH THE WORLD
Even before quests are implemented, habitat placement can foreshadow gameplay:
- Dogkie teaches forest navigation/ground combat.
- Fish makes water feel alive and later supports swimming progression.
- Horse rewards open-land exploration and future traversal/climbing-adjacent routes.
- Phoenix gives meaning to vertical world design and high-altitude flight.
