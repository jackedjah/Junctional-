## B. PROJECT ARCHITECTURE (as it actually is)

MAHFITT has **no bundler and no build step**. Client code is plain static ES modules with cache-busting
query strings; the production HTML shell is generated by `netlify/functions/mygym.js`. The 3D renderer
is served the same way and is opened from any static server rooted at the repository root.

**Production is untouched by all of this.** The shipped Mr. Mah is still the 2.5D inline-SVG rig in
`netlify/functions/mygym.js` (`fabiRigHTML()`), styled by the `MR.MAH` block in `mygym.css`. Nothing
in production imports `mrmah3d/`. Mounting the 3D renderer into any member-facing surface is a
product decision that has NOT been taken (CLAUDE.md §2).

## E. THE NEXT PASS — ANATOMICAL CLOSURE AS A SYSTEM

The next pass is deeper than surface anatomy. Build the character as if the musculature under the
crystal were visible, in this order and never the reverse: skeletal / anatomical structure → muscle
origin and insertion relationships → major muscle-belly volume → secondary separation → silhouette →
smooth-clay validation → facets following the anatomy → black / platinum value → cyan last.

What Astra must hold in mind, region by region, with where it lives in this code base:

- **Skeletal landmarks.** The character has no skeleton object; the "skeleton" is the RING TABLE's
  y-positions and depths (clavicle line 2.092–2.120, shoulder line 2.120, pec crown 1.935, under-pec
  shelf 1.830, ribcage bottom ~1.78, belt 1.480, the quad apex row, the knee rows, the point 0) plus
  the arm joint positions in ARMS (`shoulder`, `elbow`, `wrist`) and the deltoid axis
  (`ARMS.deltoid`). Set these FIRST, from the sheets' side and front views; every belly hangs off
  them. The neck column must be narrower than its collar and the head seats into the collar by 0.03
  (R101 law).
- **Origins and insertions.** Each belly is a shape lobe on specific rings (a column event) or a
  limb-profile bulge at a specific t. A muscle has to START and END: the pec's lobe grows from the
  clavicle-groove row (`pecK` ramp) to the crown and falls into an under-pec crease with `cav`; the
  deltoid's anterior lobe must overlap the upper pec and its distal end must sit INSIDE the arm tube
  (a cap that ends at the joint leaves a disc); the biceps peaks a third of the way down and narrows
  before the elbow; the lat's upper border sweeps diagonally from the armpit to the spine; the glute
  originates at a sacral shelf and inserts into the hamstring flow. In code these are `chestShape /
  coreShape / subclavicleShape / trapTerms / backTerms` amplitudes per ring, `shapes.upper /
  shapes.fore` per t, the deltoid `segment`'s profile and zone, and `thighShape / hipShape` lobes.
- **Belly direction and curvature.** Use `dome()` (compact support) and `belly()` for bellies, never a
  Gaussian smear; a COLUMN step (around the ring) may be large (0.3–0.5 of the ring), a ROW step (ring
  to ring) must stay small or it reads as a plate seam (CLAUDE.md, "Anatomy is authored at two
  amplitudes"). The pec's forward depth belongs in its FRONT lobe, never in the ring's `d`, or the back
  bulges with it (the current back ledge).
- **Overlap and continuity.** Shoulder-to-arm: the cap must read as three heads that taper to the
  insertion and overlap the arm — the arm must not be as thick as the cap. Chest-to-arm: the pec's
  lateral insertion runs under the anterior deltoid. Lat-to-waist: the lat block is HELD for two rings
  then pinched (never funnelled). Continuity is bought with shadow between masses (the underarm
  pocket, the under-pec crease) as much as with geometry.
- **Abdominal wall.** Three paired rectus bellies as domes in BOTH directions (around the ring and
  along y across two authored rows each), a linea alba cleft below the ring's nominal radius, narrow
  crease rows carrying `cav`, obliques as a diagonal sheet into the belt, serratus digitations on the
  ribcage. They must exist in CLAY (`?debug=clay`); the class tables only shade them.
- **Back layering.** Trapezius kite spread across the girdle rings; rear deltoid lobes; scapular
  planes; the lats' diagonal upper border; erector columns beside a spine channel; a monotonic depth
  from the shoulder line to the waist (no ring wider than both neighbours anywhere along the back).
- **Glute / upper-thigh.** Male: restrained glute lobes but REAL hamstring columns and a fold behind,
  so the rear reads as anatomy, not an egg. Female: a sacral shelf row, two full posterior masses as
  `dome` pairs with a two-vertex cleft a third of their height deep, lateral sweep continuing into the
  thigh, a fold only at the back, hamstring domes continuing the posterior line.
- **Lower teardrop.** ONE loft, monotonic outline below the apex, the knee as a change of slope held
  by two rows 0.02 apart with `cav`, the shin nearly parallel, then the straight cone; calf anatomy
  only as facet flow (`fg` strips and `columns`), value (class rows) and the internal light — never as
  outline.
- **Neck.** A column ~0.05 of height rising from the collar, the trapezius climbing to meet it at ~40°.

The result must be one coherent anatomical system: change a landmark and the bellies that hang off it
move with it. Prefer semantic controls (the existing shape-lobe parameters, `pecK`, `latK`, `erectorK`,
`trapK`, the ARMS spec) over new magic numbers; add a named parameter when a control does not exist.
