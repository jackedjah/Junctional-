# MAHWORLD — WORLD 01 / CLAUDE WORLD BUILD

Project Brain + Low-Usage Autonomous Build Directive

Purpose: persistent world-build memory for Claude/Astra-style coding agents.
Primary use: read this file at the beginning of every MAHWORLD World 01 session
instead of re-explaining the entire vision.

---

## 1. PRIMARY GOAL

Continue from the newest legitimate MAHWORLD World 01 implementation. Do not restart the map.

The immediate visual goal is to transform the current city/plaza from a dense, angular, noisy
futuristic environment into a spacious, premium, smooth, rounded, highly readable world with long
sightlines, large calm negative spaces, deliberate landmarks, and extremely polished metallic
materials. The world should feel expensive and authored.

The player must be able to stand near a building, turn away from it, and see open world depth
rather than another wall of geometry.

The target is CITY + WORLD, not BUILDINGS PACKED INTO EVERY VIEW.

## 2. CURRENT PRIORITY — SPACIOUSNESS

Highest-priority World 01 correction. The world must create the feeling of large physical distance.

When the player looks away from a facility, across the main plaza, between buildings, toward the
skyline, mountains, water, the Ascent, or a distant district, the eye should be able to travel
through the scene. Do not fill every gap.

Create deliberate: open plazas, broad pedestrian fields, landscaped gaps, water corridors, mountain
sightlines, distant skyline openings, long curved paths, quiet reflective ground, large breathing
zones around landmarks.

DENSITY RULE — a view should generally contain: (1) one dominant visual destination, (2) a small
number of supporting structures, (3) open visual breathing room, (4) distant layers.
Do not make every visible square meter a landmark. Negative space is part of the architecture.

## 3. WORLD DEPTH

Every important gameplay view should read as layers.

- FOREGROUND premium reflective paving; subtle seams / diamond organization; rails, benches,
  planters or docking pieces only where useful; restrained lighting; clear walking space.
- NEAR FIELD a few MAHBEINGS; FOB-derived transport; authored street objects; strong path readability.
- MIDGROUND one or two major facilities; central monuments; water / terraces / transit; trees /
  nature where appropriate.
- BACKGROUND separated tower groups; mountain gaps; water corridors; rare megatalls; distant
  districts; moon / sky.
- UPPER FIELD smooth FOBEAMS; sky roads; floating infrastructure; MAHGIC routes; Ascent paths.

Never flatten the whole world into one continuous wall of buildings.

## 4. ARCHITECTURAL FORM LANGUAGE

Move strongly toward ROUND + SMOOTH + PREMIUM.

PREFER large continuous curves; softened tower corners; rounded shells; flowing concourses;
circular / oval plazas; smooth terraces; rounded cross-form structures; filleted beams; sculpted
transitions; seamless podiums; smooth ramps; graceful sky-road arcs; clean pod geometry; broad
polished surfaces.

AVOID random spikes; repeated sharp shards; primitive boxes; tiny triangles scattered everywhere;
overly faceted facades; thin unsupported geometry; arbitrary crystal clutter; buildings that feel
generated from repeated primitives; harsh angular transitions where a premium radius would work.

Diamond geometry remains important to MAHWORLD, but it is an IDENTITY/DETAIL language, not an
excuse to make every object sharp. Think: smooth body + precise MAH diamond accents.

## 5. MATERIAL STANDARD

Primary material family: platinum; polished silver; chromium; liquid-silver; dark reflective
crystal; restrained glass; controlled luminous MAHGIC accents.

USE smooth high-polish reflections; controlled roughness variation; broad highlight bands; strong
dark/light separation; believable reflections; subtle material depth.

AVOID everything glowing equally; flat gray; cheap white plastic; excessive bloom; noisy microscopic
facets; random neon trim on every edge.

A structure should still look expensive with its glow disabled.

## 6. CENTRAL MR. MAH + MRS. MAH MONUMENT

The two central figures are a major World 01 identity landmark, and must be treated as
representations of the ACTUAL Mr. Mah and Mrs. Mah, not generic humanoid sculptures.

Both statues must use canonical MAH diamond-head identity; canonical MAH anatomy; canonical fused
lower-body / teardrop structure; recognizable Mr. Mah / Mrs. Mah proportions; premium sculpted
muscle structure; believable front, side and rear silhouette; intentional hands / arms; actual
sex-specific silhouette differences.

MR. MAH — powerful broad upper body; strong chest; strong shoulders / arms; developed core; large
thigh/lower-form anatomy; canonical head / facial proportion language.

MRS. MAH — unmistakable female canonical silhouette; strong upper curvature; narrow waist
relationship; substantial hips / thighs; anatomically authored fused lower body; canonical head /
facial proportion language.

Do not use ordinary independent human legs if current canonical character geometry uses the fused
MAH lower construction. Do not settle for low-detail mannequin bodies.

## 7. MONUMENT MATERIAL

Premium silver monuments: high-polish platinum / liquid silver / chrome sculpture, not white stone.
Deep reflections; bright but controlled specular bands; strong readable silhouettes; dark reflected
regions; premium metal response; no chalk-white plastic; no flat diffuse statue shader.

Readable at night through carefully placed ENVIRONMENT LIGHTING rather than self-emissive bodies.

## 8. MONUMENT COMPOSITION

Deliberate breathing room. Preserve / develop monumental scale; broad negative space around the
figures; circular or tiered premium pedestal; controlled water / reflective integration where
useful; upward relational pose; shared glowing square-diamond / MAHGIC focal object if retained;
vertical light / MAHGIC column if retained; "HIGHER TOGETHER" identity where appropriate;
controlled ground light pool.

Visually important because of scale, silhouette, material and placement — not because hundreds of
effects surround it.

## 9. BUILDING PLACEMENT / SPACING LAW

Do not solve empty space by adding another building. Before adding geometry ask:
> Does this space need an object, or does this space need to remain open so the world can breathe?

Use building clusters separated by meaningful gaps. Major facilities should feel like actual
destinations rather than storefronts touching one another.

Create approach space; entry forecourts; plazas; landscaped buffers; transit zones; water gaps;
paths around buildings; rear service / world space; sight corridors between districts.

A building should be viewable as a complete object from some positions.

## 10. MAH GYM / MAH MATCH / MAH MARKET

- MAH GYM wide; flowing; open; platinum + glass; visible training space; aspirational; smooth.
- MAH MATCH strongest combat identity; rounded armored cross-core; clear arena / practice-round
  feeling; strong but not spiky; heavy premium shell.
- MAH MARKET civic / social / commercial; terraced; curved transparent frontage; warmer activity;
  welcoming public scale.

All share smooth premium radii; physical thickness; real entrances; real ramps / stairs / terraces;
premium shell materials; restrained MAHGIC; readable signage; actual interior depth.

## 11. NATURE + WATER + HORIZON

MAHWORLD must not become an endless cyberpunk city canyon. Preserve and expand mountain visibility;
water corridors; waterfalls where appropriate; trees / designed landscape; open sky; distant
horizons; calm environmental regions.

Architecture should FRAME nature rather than erase it. Important views should sometimes allow:
ground -> water/nature -> distant architecture -> mountains -> sky.

## 12. FOBEAMS / SKY SYSTEMS

Smoother and more elegant: graceful curved trajectories; thin-to-medium routes; asynchronous packet
movement; physical endpoints; clear depth layering; restrained bloom; fewer visually overpowering
giant arcs. Do not use sky systems to fill every empty region. Empty sky is also valuable.

interactive inspection.

## 22. FLOOR TEXT / SURFACE-LEGIBILITY RULE

Any intentional floor text, directional marking, branding, slogan, facility identifier, progression
marker, or wayfinding typography must render at the FOREFRONT of the finished floor surface and
remain clearly readable during normal gameplay.

Use whichever Three.js-compatible method best preserves quality and avoids z-fighting: render-order
/ depth priority; a tiny physical vertical offset above the finished floor; properly layered decal /
projected material logic; polygon offset; dedicated floor-marking mesh/material; depthWrite /
depthTest choices only where they do not create incorrect world overlap; texture or material
compositing when cleaner than stacked meshes.

DO NOT ALLOW text hidden under floor panels; text underneath reflective overlays; flickering /
z-fighting; blur caused by excessive reflection; unreadable low-contrast lettering; text visibly
floating far above the floor; text clipping through characters or props.

Desired illusion: the text is physically integrated into the TOPMOST finished floor layer —
engraved, inlaid, printed, illuminated, or laminated into the premium ground while remaining crisp.
Evaluate at close walking distance; normal third-person distance; shallow viewing angles; plaza hero.

## 23. COLOR-CORRECTION + CONTRAST SYSTEM

World 01 is no longer allowed to collapse into one dark-blue / white monochrome field.

NEUTRAL PREMIUM FOUNDATION (dominant): premium silver; soft metallic gray; graphite; near-black;
black crystal; deep navy / blue-black; cool white.

CONTROLLED ACCENT FAMILY (selective): purple; sky blue / cyan-blue; clean red. Appearing in facility
identity; MAHGIC accents; transit indicators; selected windows; signage; focal lighting; character
themes; controlled environment details; event / combat / social zones.

STRICT EXCLUSIONS — the world must not read yellow; orange; amber; copper; bronze; warm golden;
reddish-orange. Red is allowed only as a clean intentional red accent, never orange-red / warm
amber contamination. No warm sunset-like grading should dominate the canonical World 01 palette.

CONTRAST GOAL — pop through value separation; metal-vs-crystal separation; dark-vs-light hierarchy;
restrained accent color; controlled emissive intensity; localized rather than global saturation.
Do not solve "more color" by making every surface bright.
Use: neutral premium mass + selective color events.

## 24. COLOR OWNERSHIP

graphite / black crystal = depth, structural recess, shadow mass. silver / platinum = premium hero
shell. cool white = high-value light / clean interface / civic clarity. sky blue = traversal /
network / calm MAHGIC / infrastructure. purple = social / cosmic / premium secondary energy.
red = combat / warning / intensity / MAH MATCH emphasis.

Do not hard-code these so rigidly that future theme systems become impossible. Use a reusable world
palette / token system where practical.

## 25. THREE.JS IMPLEMENTATION LAW

SCENE OWNERSHIP — clear boundaries: geometry factories; materials; lighting; environment / sky;
post-processing; camera / controls; decals / floor markings; animation / MAHGIC effects; loading /
disposal; evidence / diagnostic camera utilities. Do not bury unrelated behavior in one giant file.

MATERIALS — prefer MeshStandardMaterial / MeshPhysicalMaterial; environment reflections; roughness /
metalness discipline; clearcoat only when justified; transmission only where appropriate and
performance-safe. Avoid excessive custom shader complexity for effects achievable with standard
materials; emissive-only fake metal; flat Lambert-like treatment for premium chrome; huge numbers of
unique materials when parameters/textures can be reused.

REFLECTIONS — suitable environment map / PMREM workflow; avoid uniformly perfect mirrors; use
roughness variation; preserve dark reflected values so form remains readable; avoid blown-out white
clipping. Balance against mobile performance.

COLOR MANAGEMENT — correct renderer color management / output for the project's Three.js version.
No accidental double conversion. Consistent color-space handling; texture color-space assignments;
tone mapping; exposure. Solve color correction systematically, not by per-object brightness hacks.

LIGHTING — hierarchy: environmental / hemisphere contribution; intentional key lights; local
landmark lights; restrained emissive accents. Avoid dozens of overlapping high-intensity lights.
The statues need controlled key/rim/environment response so polished silver anatomy stays readable.

GEOMETRY — intentional radii / bevels / fillets; adequate segment counts only where silhouette needs
them; reuse instanced or shared geometry; avoid needless subdivision on invisible faces; preserve
normals; repair normals after procedural deformation.

CURVES / FOBEAMS / SKY ROADS — smooth curves with sensible sampling. Avoid extreme segment counts;
visibly faceted tubes; random spaghetti trajectories; giant tube radii that dominate the sky.

Z-FIGHTING — resolve coincident surfaces intentionally. For floor text especially: avoid coplanar
geometry; use tiny offsets / polygonOffset / material compositing; confirm from moving cameras.

TRANSPARENCY — sparingly. Sort / depth issues must be visually tested. Do not stack many transparent
surfaces in important vistas if opaque / alpha-tested / composited achieves the same more reliably.

PERFORMANCE — mobile/PWA compatible. Instancing for repeated props; geometry reuse; texture reuse;
frustum culling; distance-based simplification; LOD; pooled/reused effects; bounded particle counts;
disposal of superseded GPU resources; targeted shadow casters; controlled pixel ratio.
Do not improve one screenshot by making roaming unusable.

SHADOWS — intentional. Hero landmarks may deserve higher quality. Not every tiny prop casts.

ASSET LIFECYCLE — dispose obsolete GPU resources when no longer reused; avoid leaking render targets
/ textures / geometries; preserve asset caches intentionally.

ANIMATION — delta-time-correct. Do not tie motion speed to frame rate. Coherent update ownership
rather than many uncontrolled RAF loops.

RESPONSIVE RENDERING — update camera aspect; update renderer size; avoid unnecessary reallocations;
respect device pixel ratio policy; functional on phone / tablet dimensions.

EVIDENCE GATE — after meaningful visual changes: check console errors; check WebGL warnings; check
camera movement; check mobile-scale performance; check at least the diagnostic camera relevant.

## 26. DOME / MEGA-SCALE SPACIOUSNESS

The dome and large enclosing regions must use the spaciousness target at EXTREME scale: perceived
enormity; vertical distance; long empty spans; tiny player-to-world scale relationship; massive
architectural silhouettes separated by atmospheric space; bridges / routes crossing enormous voids;
layered depth; water / nature / architecture at world scale.

Do NOT copy external reference architecture. Extract only scale relationships; negative space;
depth; atmosphere; framing; large-vs-small contrast; long-distance composition.

DOME RULE — the dome must not feel like a ceiling sitting just above the city. It should feel like
an enormous containing world shell. From appropriate locations the player should perceive immense
height; major empty air volume; distant structures separated by huge space; atmospheric haze; large
suspended / elevated routes; deep world layers; long uninterrupted views. The player should
sometimes appear visually tiny against the environment.

INTERIOR SPACING — resist constant building density; create giant traversable voids; use rare
monumental structures; allow waterfalls / nature / distant architecture; use long bridges or Ascent
connections across large distances; reveal other districts far away rather than immediately nearby.
The space should inspire "I can actually go over there", not "everything is stacked around me".
