THE DEPARTURE: the canonical identity colour is champagne gold #E9C98F (every opaque pixel of images/mahfitt-mark-gold.png is exactly that value), and MAHWORLD's own light law LAW-001 forbids it — hue 38.7 at 0.67 saturation. Both concept images render the facility names in cool white and the reference manifest recorded this in v3, so the world takes every canonical property EXCEPT the hue, in the one cream that passes (#EFEAE0). BRAND.gold is kept in materials.js as the record; relaxing the light law for signage is a one-line change.

The phone hero bands correctly: platinum floor in the lower third, then the facilities and residents, then the background districts, then mountains, FOBEAMs, moon and open sky. THE TENSION, stated rather than hidden: §05 (real spacing between the facilities) and §46 (all of them inside one portrait frame) pull against each other at a physical camera. The hero therefore makes MAH MATCH the subject and lets MAH GYM and MAH MARKET enter at the frame edges. The concept image resolves this with an illustrative composition rather than a perspective camera.

Remaining visible discrepancies

  • The fifteen midground city blocks are still one geometry recipe at fifteen scales. Material and depth variety were added around them; their massing was not.
  • The three destination bodies and the block masses remain dark on purpose, as the value floor the platinum reads against. The brief's platinum-first target is met on the floor, frontages, canopies, aprons and furniture but not yet on the primary elevations.
  • Near-camera vegetation is still six planters over the whole deck; the new planting is mid-distance.
  • The reserved square-diamond mark slot on the three signs is still empty: no verified FOB or MAHFITT mark file has been supplied.
  • Banner copy shown in the concept image (FIGHT HIGHER, LIVE HIGHER, SKILL BUILDS PEOPLE, HUMAN POTENTIAL LIVES HERE, CONNECT TRAIN COMPETE SHOP BELONG) is NOT in the world: none of it was verifiable in the app source, and inventing slogans is outside what this work may do. TRAIN HIGHER is present because it already existed in the scene.
  • Frame times come from headless software GL on a shared 4-core machine and are inflated; only draw calls, triangles, lights and shadow-caster counts are device-independent.
  • The phone hero cannot contain all three facilities at a realistic lens now that they are genuinely separated; MAH MATCH is the portrait subject and the other two enter at the edges. See gate §45/§46.
